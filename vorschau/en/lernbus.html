Pillar · The Learning Bus

The Learning Bus

The Learning Bus drives to where the children are, right into the neighbourhood. Inside sits one child with a mentor who is there for this one child alone. The mentor sees where the child is today, and goes on from there with them.

Starting where the child is

Every child is at a different point. The mentor looks closely first and finds the task that fits right now, not too easy and not too hard. When the child manages it, the mentor says so straight away and warmly. That way the child stays with it, and notices for themselves how, time after time, they are getting a little better.

The same person, over a long time

With the Learning Bus, it is always the same mentor. Out of the meetings a relationship grows, one in which the child dares to take a chance, to make mistakes, and to ask. There is someone who knows the child and believes they can do this. A screen can praise a child without end, but it cannot believe in them. That is exactly what matters to us.

A book that belongs to the child

At the end, a bound book is made for each child, with photographs, drawings, and their own sentences. It holds what the child worked on, and what they thought and asked. Nothing is marked, everything is kept. For many children it is the first thing that shows: I thought of this myself. The book belongs to the child.

Open to all, made possible for many

Families can book the Learning Bus. So that children whose family cannot carry the cost can take part too, we fund places through investment. When you invest in a place, you give a child time with a person who believes in them.

A girl is working out a problem that was still too hard for her last week. She pauses. The mentor waits and says nothing. Then the answer comes from her, all by herself. She looks up, the mentor just nods, and she beams, because this time she managed it on her own.
