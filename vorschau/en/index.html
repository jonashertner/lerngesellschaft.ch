What this is about

Curiosity is the most precious thing a child brings with them.

Every child wants, all on their own, to understand how the world works. And every child wants to be heard and understood.

Today, a great deal is simply consumed, glanced at and swiped away. But a society only moves forward when children's curiosity is kept alive and learning is something they experience, try out, and explore. Switzerland's national assessment of basic competences shows how urgent this is: already in the second year of primary school, almost one child in four does not reach basic competence in mathematics, and in reading it is just over one in five.

This is where we begin. Through three pillars we invest in the children of today, actively and before gaps take hold, and in a future rich in learning and connection. So that as many children as possible can take part, we rely, as an association, on people who invest alongside us.

Our goals

What we are working towards.

01

Keep curiosity alive

Children stay curious and keep learning all their lives, because they want to, not because they have to.

02

Experience science

Children feel how exciting it is to follow a question of their own, and gain confidence and joy in learning.

03

Understand ourselves and others

Children come to understand themselves and their feelings, and treat one another with care.

The three pillars

Three paths, one aim.

We awaken wonder, we go with each child at their own pace, and we show how learning, and getting along well together, can work.
