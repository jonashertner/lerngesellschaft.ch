Privacy at a glance

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are loaded from our own website hosting. An email is sent only when you send it from your email application.

For appointment booking, we link to Microsoft Bookings. An embedded calendar loads only when you select “Show appointments”. The booking provider then receives technical access data, including your IP address. Information entered into the calendar is processed by that provider to organise the conversation. You can contact us by email or post instead.

Contact and enquiries

The Lernbus page offers a contact form and an email link. Both open your own email application; the form prepares your message there. The website does not store contact details you enter. The message is sent only when you send it from your email application.

Enquiry forms on the Forscherbühne pages also only assemble an email from your entries. Those entries are not transmitted to or stored on this website. The message is sent only when you send it from your email application.

Who gets to see your enquiry

Our mailboxes run on Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Corporation). Your email is processed and held there, and that processing also takes place outside Switzerland, including in the United States. If you would rather avoid that, please write to us by post at the address above.

Enquiries are read only by people in the association who are dealing with them. We pass them on to nobody and use them for nothing other than your enquiry.

How long we keep them

An enquiry stays in our mailbox while we are dealing with it and is deleted afterwards. If the enquiry turns into an ongoing engagement, we keep the correspondence for as long as that engagement lasts.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). As with any web server, technical access data arises: IP address, time, page requested. That data is processed by the host; we do not evaluate it and have no access to it.

Your rights

You can ask at any time what data we hold about you and demand its correction or deletion. A line to team@jonashertner.com is enough. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can turn to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Images and texts

The texts and designs on this website belong to the Lerngesellschaft. If you would like to use any of it, just ask. We almost always say yes.
