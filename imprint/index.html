Website

No analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are hosted locally. GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) processes technical data such as IP address, time and page requested to deliver the website.

Enquiries

We use your details only to handle your enquiry. Access is limited to the people responsible within the association.

Contact form: Formspree processes your name, email, message and technical data in the USA to deliver messages and protect the service. After you select “Send message”, your enquiry is forwarded to info@lernbus.ch and temporarily stored in a submission archive. Formspree privacy policy.

Email: Processed and stored through Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Corporation), including in the USA.

You can also contact us directly by email or post. Email links and Forscherbühne enquiries open your email application; you send the message yourself.

Bookings

The calendar checks available times through our booking service hosted by OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. When you reserve a time, your name, email address, appointment and optional notes are sent to Microsoft Bookings, including for processing in the USA. Our booking service stores technical reservation identifiers and appointment times for up to 90 days after the appointment; unresolved reservations are retained until resolved. Names, email addresses and notes are not permanently stored there. Your entries are temporarily kept in your browser's session storage so that an interrupted reservation can be recovered safely.

Retention & rights

Enquiries in our mailbox are deleted once handled. If we work together, correspondence is retained until that engagement ends.

Request access, correction or deletion at team@jonashertner.com. Complaints: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.
