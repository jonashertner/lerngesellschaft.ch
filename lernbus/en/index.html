Individual early learning support that helps children grow strong.

Lernbus is an early learning programme grounded in sound educational principles. It supports children before difficulties arise.

Lernbus helps children shape their own development with confidence, independence and a focus on the future.

Why Lernbus

Learning begins before letters and numbers.

That is why we look at what children need before they begin to struggle with reading, writing or maths.

What learning builds on

Learning builds on many fundamental abilities: perception, attention, language, early mathematical skills, motor skills and self-regulation.

How we support early learning

Learning through play

Children can build these foundations early, through play, with enjoyment and without pressure. Often, it happens naturally, without feeling like ‘learning’. Whether they are making things, moving, sorting, rhyming, experimenting or playing, there are countless ways to support their development and strengthen the foundations for learning.

A stable foundation

For us, early learning support means giving children a secure foundation for later learning, rather than bringing formal schooling forward. We do not wait for gaps to become visible.

Our approach and goals

Lernbus focuses on core skills such as emotional intelligence, creativity, communication, critical thinking and the ability to make connections.

Thinking for themselves

Children learn to think for themselves, recognise connections, find their own ways to solve problems and learn from mistakes.

Learning to persevere

They learn strategies for engaging with topics that may not immediately interest them and for keeping going when they feel reluctant.

Core skills and early German language support

At the same time, we can address gaps in basic language and maths skills and strengthen these foundations.

Early German language support helps children from age three build language foundations, become more confident in expressing themselves and feel more at ease taking part in lessons.

Confidence and resilience

The programme strengthens children's self-confidence, their resilience and their readiness to learn independently.

The goals

  • Strengthening learning skills, self-worth and self-efficacy
  • Fostering curiosity and intrinsic motivation to learn
  • Building resilience and strategies for dealing with setbacks
  • Creating a safe learning environment where children feel valued
  • Fostering communication skills and independent thinking
  • Making individual learning progress visible

My background

I have worked with children in nurseries since I was 16. I began as a nursery practitioner, then spent 14 years running Kita Schnäggi as a self-employed co-director.

More about Conny

My professional development has included training to supervise apprentices and to support early language development, with a focus on German. I am also the mother of two grown-up children and an eight-year-old.

Making time for early learning

Our society still gives too little recognition to the importance of early education and support. In 30 years of working with children, I have found that one thing matters above all when helping each child develop their individual abilities: time.

Time for a child to try things out. And time for an adult to help them practise and strengthen fundamental skills, through play, with warmth and professional knowledge.

Individual support

At Lernbus, we create that space and time, giving children individual support to strengthen their core skills.

Every child, like every person, is unique. No single approach will help every child persevere when something is not working out. But with time, professional knowledge and a caring approach, we can work with each child to discover what they need and help them make it part of how they learn.

How it works

  • Introductory meeting with the parents and the child

  • Individual early learning support in 60-minute lessons. One to one in the first semester to build a trusting relationship, then in pairs in the second semester. Booked for one school semester at a time.

  • Building fundamental abilities: perception, attention, language, early mathematical skills, motor skills and self-regulation

  • A safe learning environment built on trust

  • A personal learning box (Lernkiste) to document each child's progress

  • Parent meetings and support tailored to the needs of children and parents, including accompanying them to meetings

  • Small groups during school holidays to strengthen and develop a wide range of skills

Frequently asked questions

Is this tutoring?

Not in the usual sense. We address gaps in learning where needed. Above all, we help your child enjoy learning and become an independent learner. That lasts beyond any single topic.

Does my child need to be struggling?

No. Lernbus is open to every child, whatever their starting point or background. We begin early to preserve and strengthen their confidence and enjoyment of learning.

Is it too early to start?

Young children learn through play and through secure relationships. That is where we begin: helping them enjoy learning from the outset, in many different ways.

Why book for a school semester?

Because trust takes time. Progress comes from a reliable relationship and enthusiasm for each child and their learning, rather than from any single exercise. Lessons are booked for a school semester at a time.

Who works with my child?

Our mentors have training in education and experience of working with children. They are carefully selected to meet the highest standards. Regular team meetings, shared professional learning, evaluation and continuing professional development help us maintain quality.

Meet the team →

One-to-one lessons or lessons in pairs?

In the first semester, lessons are one to one. This gives your child and their mentor time to build a trusting relationship. In the second semester, lessons take place in pairs. You book one school semester at a time.

And during the school holidays?

During the school holidays, small groups help children who attend Lernbus build on and consolidate what they have learnt during the school semester.

Professional standards and quality assurance

Our mentors bring empathy, reflective practice and professional expertise to their work. Their educational training and experience enable them to respond to each child's interests and needs.

We maintain and continually improve the quality of the programme.
