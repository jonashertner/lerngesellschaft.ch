01 / The idea

Curiosity grows
in relationship.

Children arrive in the world full of questions. Lasting learning happens when they feel themselves moving forward, and when someone who knows them stays alongside them and trusts what they can do.

In an age of instant answers and artificial intelligence, genuine understanding matters more than ever. Digital tools can support. Relationship, judgement and pedagogical responsibility remain human.

Read our approach→

  • A relationship that lasts

    Consistent support from the same person across months and school semesters.

  • Progress that builds courage

    Tasks calibrated to the point where a child’s own effort visibly pays off.

  • Open to every child

    Income-tiered fees and subsidised places regardless of background or means.

02 / Programmes & initiatives

Grounded in Basel.
Step by step.

Our first programme, Lernbus, already supports children at Münsterplatz. Two further initiatives for schools and the wider public are currently in development.

01Active
Lernbus For children from age three to the end of primary year three

Consistent, long-term learning support and early German language development at Münsterplatz 17. Weekly, individually or in a small group, connecting family, kindergarten and school.

03 / People & association

Carried by people
in Basel.

Lerngesellschaft / Learning Society is a non-profit, politically independent and denominationally neutral Swiss association (Art. 60 ff. Swiss Civil Code) based in Basel.

It brings together people from education, natural sciences and law. The board serves voluntarily. As a charitable association, Lerngesellschaft is tax-exempt in the canton of Basel-Stadt.
