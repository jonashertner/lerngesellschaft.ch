Imprint & privacy

Responsible body, association details and factual information on how personal data is handled on this website and when you contact us.

Website

No analytics or advertising trackers are used on this website. Fonts and images are loaded locally. GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) processes technical connection data such as IP address, timestamp and requested page in order to deliver the site.

Enquiries

Your details are used solely to handle your enquiry. Access is limited to the responsible people within the association.

Contact form (Lernbus): Formspree processes in the USA your name, email address, phone number, number and ages of your children, any optional provisional reservation for Zwischenhalt, your optional message, and technical data needed to transmit and protect the service.

When submitted via the Lernbus enquiry form, your enquiry is forwarded to {{ lernbusContact.recipient | escape }} and temporarily stored in the form archive. Formspree privacy policy.

Email: Processed and stored via Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Corporation), including in the USA.

You can also contact us directly by email or post. Email links open your own email application; you send the message yourself.

WhatsApp contact with Lernbus

If you contact Lernbus via WhatsApp, we process your phone number, profile name, and the messages and attachments you send us in order to answer your enquiry and organise learning support.

The responsible members of the Lernbus team can read and reply to this correspondence in our shared inbox. We host that inbox with Chatwoot on our own hardware and use Tailscale for encrypted connectivity.

Transmission takes place via the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by WhatsApp/Meta, which may involve processing data outside Switzerland, in particular in the USA. The WhatsApp privacy notice also applies.

Using WhatsApp is voluntary; you may also contact us by email or post. Please share particularly sensitive details about your child only after agreeing on a suitable channel.

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your Lernbus correspondence at info@lernbus.ch.

Earlier calendar bookings

The contact form replaces the earlier booking calendar.

For earlier reservations made via our booking service on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare, your name, email address and phone number, your child’s name and date of birth, the age calculated from it, the appointment time and any optional notes were transmitted to Microsoft Bookings, including for processing in the USA.

Our booking service stores technical reservation identifiers and appointment times for up to 90 days after the appointment; unresolved reservations remain stored until clarified.

Contact details, child details and notes are not stored permanently there. To allow interrupted reservations to resume safely, entries were temporarily kept in the browser’s session storage.

Retention & rights

Enquiries in our inbox are deleted once concluded; where collaboration begins, correspondence is retained until it ends, subject to statutory retention obligations.

You may request access, correction or deletion at team@jonashertner.com. Supervisory authority for complaints: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
