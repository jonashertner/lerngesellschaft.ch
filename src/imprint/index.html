Privacy at a glance

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are loaded from our own website hosting. Details entered in the Lernbus contact form are transmitted only when you choose “Send message”.

Contact and enquiries

The contact forms on the German and English Lernbus main pages send your message over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States). Formspree processes your name, email address and message, forwards the enquiry to info@lernbus.ch and temporarily retains it in a submission archive. Technical information such as your IP address is also processed to provide and protect the service. Processing takes place in the United States, among other locations. For details, see Formspree’s privacy policy.

You can contact us directly by email or post instead. Email links and the enquiry forms on the Forscherbühne pages open your own email application. Those messages are sent only when you send them yourself.

Who gets to see your enquiry

We use Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Corporation) for email. Your emails are processed and stored there, including outside Switzerland and in the United States. You can write to us by post at the address above if you prefer.

Enquiries are read only by people in the association who are dealing with them. We pass them on to nobody and use them for nothing other than your enquiry.

How long we keep them

We keep your enquiry in our mailbox while we deal with it, then delete it. If it leads to an ongoing engagement, we retain the correspondence for the duration of that engagement.

Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). As with any web server, the host processes technical access data, including your IP address, the time of your visit and the page requested. We do not analyse or have access to this data.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you and request its correction or deletion at any time. Email team@jonashertner.com to make a request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Images and text

The text and designs on this website belong to Lerngesellschaft. Please ask if you would like to use them. We almost always say yes.
