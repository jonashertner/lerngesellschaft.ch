Pillar · Forscherbühne

Forscherbühne

On the Forscherbühne, a real person shows real science live, right in front of the children. They are amazed, and out of that wonder comes the wish to understand. Everything is free, for children and young people in Basel.

Wonder you share

Someone special performs a real experiment, here in the room and not on a screen. Something bangs, lights up, or moves, and no one quite expected it. In that moment a child asks, all on their own: how does that work? That question is the beginning of real understanding.

Someone who loves what they do

At the front stands someone who has mastered their subject and loves it. This person shows real experiments, no tricks and no show. The children sense that there is real skill behind the wonder, and that you can come to understand things that look at first like magic.

Free, for children and young people

The Forscherbühne costs nothing, and every child is welcome, wherever they come from. We invite children and young people, gladly together with their families and schools. Wonder is something to share, with a real person in the same room.

A girl sits in the front row. At the front, someone pours two clear liquids together, and suddenly it glows blue. She gasps and looks up at her father. Then she turns back and asks, quietly: how does that work? She takes that question home with her.
