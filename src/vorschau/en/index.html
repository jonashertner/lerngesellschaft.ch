What this is about

Curiosity is the most precious thing a child brings with them.

Every child wants, all on their own, to understand how the world works. And every child wants to be heard and understood.

That curiosity grows when a child feels they are getting better, step by step, and when there is someone there who believes they can do it.

This is what we nurture in Basel, through three pillars. When you invest here, you help many children find this place, and you help it stay for years to come.

Our goals

What we are working towards.

01

Keep curiosity alive

Keep a child's natural curiosity alive, so that learning for life becomes something they live gladly and shape for themselves.

02

Experience science

Make science and experiment something children can experience, and so build curiosity, confidence, and the joy of learning.

03

Understand ourselves and others

Understand how we ourselves and other people work, so that living together is thoughtful and goes well.

The three pillars

Three paths, one aim.

We awaken wonder, we go with each child at their own pace, and we show how learning, and getting along well together, can work.
