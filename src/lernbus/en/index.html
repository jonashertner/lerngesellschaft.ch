Learning support in Basel

We start where the child is.

Lernbus supports children in Basel from the year before kindergarten to the third year of primary school. We learn in small groups, in different neighbourhood centres and at Münsterplatz.

The early years are especially valuable.

This is when children learn what everything else builds on: language, numbers, dealing with feelings and learning in a group. If we look closely early on, small difficulties can be addressed before they grow. The good news: early support happens playfully and almost in passing, through games, stories and curiosity. This keeps learning joyful and easy for children: what is consolidated early through play takes less time and energy than laboriously closing gaps later.

What Lernbus gives your child.

Confidence in dealing with feelings.

A child who can calm down when something goes wrong keeps going and is open to new things. That is why we learn to tell feelings apart and name them, and get to know different strategies for dealing with them. In this way, children find access to their feelings through language.

A good start in reading and writing.

Children who can hear rhymes and syllables, discover letters and tell lots of stories find it easier to learn to read later. With us there are many reading and letter games full of movement, fun and humour. We take up what is still difficult at school and build on the strengths your child brings.

A good start in mathematics.

Arithmetic, too, begins long before school. Children who develop a secure sense of quantities and numbers early on find things easier later. That is why we count, compare and share in the middle of play. With many different materials and games, every child finds their own way in and their own path. What is not yet secure, we practise in a focused way, so that the child can build on it later.

A reliable relationship.

Someone who has known a child for years notices early when something is not right. Trust takes time: a child only shows what they can do and what they find hard once they feel safe. Experts count such a relationship among the strongest protective factors for children. What a child has learned early needs looking after. That is why we stay with them.

Believing in them.

Children sense what others believe they can do. In every child, we first see what they can already do, and that is exactly where we start. The attitude of the adult alone influences how much a child learns. That is why we are convinced: every child brings their own great potential.

Every success becomes visible.

Throughout their time at Lernbus, every child has their own treasure chest. It fills up with the child’s personal moments of success, for example their own drawings, a symbolic object or a Lernbus card.

What we offer.

Zwischenhalt Kindergarten.

For children in the year before kindergarten. Four children and one specialist meet once a week. We practise what matters in kindergarten: joining in, asking questions, waiting, dealing with anger and disappointment, and asking for help. Early German support can also be strengthened here, in addition to the compulsory programme. On top of that there are stories, first numbers and games. The same trusted adult stays at the child’s side after kindergarten begins.

Zwischenhalt Mittag.

For children from kindergarten to the third year of primary school. Four children eat together with one specialist and learn almost in passing: with the right language prompts at the lunch table, followed by shared learning and play sessions, tailored to each child and to the group.

Lernbus lessons.

For children from kindergarten to the third year of primary school. There are no more than two children in a lesson, so topics can be approached in a very individual and focused way.

Project weeks.

From February 2027, there will also be project weeks during the school holidays, with four children per group. They follow the principle of inquiry-based learning around one topic. Children learn to open up topics for themselves: they draw on everyone’s abilities, ask questions and reach the right solution, including through what does not work.

Talking with parents.

At the end of each semester, we offer a conversation. Together we look at what has changed and what the child still needs. If things are difficult at kindergarten or school, we are happy to advise you on request. We do not replace assessments by specialists or therapy.

Fees

What does it cost?

Fees are per month and per child. There are six monthly payments from January to June and five from August to December. We discuss the appropriate rate with you at the introductory meeting. No child should miss out on an offer for financial reasons.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tutoring?

We also consolidate what your child learns at school and work on gaps where they exist. At the same time, we strengthen the foundations for learning. This helps your child enjoy learning and work independently.

Does my child need to be struggling?

No. Lernbus is open to every child, whatever their starting point or background. We begin early to preserve and strengthen their confidence and enjoyment of learning.

Is it too early to start?

Young children learn through play and through secure relationships. That is where we begin: helping them enjoy learning from the outset, in many different ways.

Why book for a school semester?

Booking for a school semester gives us time to get to know each other and keep working on suitable tasks. We combine a reliable relationship with targeted support. Lessons are booked for a school semester at a time.

Who works with my child?

Our mentors have training in education and experience of working with children. They are carefully selected. Regular team meetings, shared professional learning, evaluation and continuing professional development help us maintain quality.

Professional standards and quality assurance

Our mentors bring empathy, reflective practice and professional expertise to their work. Their educational training and experience enable them to respond to each child's interests and needs.

We maintain and continually improve the quality of the programme.

Meet the team →

Individual sessions or a small group?

Your child attends one of our offers each week, individually or in a small group. You decide what suits you and your child. Bookings are made for one school semester at a time.

And during the school holidays?

During the school holidays, there are small groups for children who attend Lernbus. Here, topics can be explored in more depth using the principle of inquiry-based learning.

Who is behind it

A programme of Lerngesellschaft.

Lerngesellschaft (Learning Society) is a non-profit association. It nurtures the natural curiosity and desire to learn of children and young people. Its board members serve on a voluntary basis.
