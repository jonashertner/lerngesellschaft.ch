The Learning Bus drives to where the children are, right into the neighbourhood. At its centre are learning competence, self-efficacy and the child's own motivation, together with the capacities that matter more and more for a successful educational path.

Learning needs visible progress

Children develop a love of learning above all when they experience that their own effort leads to success. Progress they notice themselves strengthens their confidence in their own abilities, increases their persistence, and makes them readier to take on something new. Research from Erasmus University Rotterdam indicates that people learn with the greatest concentration and persistence when they can perceive their own progress. The Learning Bus makes learning visible and creates the moments of success that sustain motivation and a positive relationship to learning.

The right challenge at the right time

Learning works best when a task matches a child's individual stage of development. The support therefore follows the child's interests, strengths and starting point. It happens in the optimal learning zone: demanding enough to prompt development, and shaped so that success remains possible. For children in primary school we speak briefly with the class teacher before we begin: what is the class working on, where is this child, what would help. Mistakes are understood as an important part of learning and used as opportunities to learn. With the youngest, before and in kindergarten, we pursue no school learning goals, we protect free play.

The same person, over a long time

With the Learning Bus, it is always the same mentor. A child comes to the same person every week, for one hour, for a whole semester (half a school year) and often longer. Screens have no place in the session: a screen interrupts this space and replaces the slow way to an answer with a quick one. The work is done with books, paper and pencils, with a magnifying glass, scales and clay, and with whatever can be found outdoors. Out of the meetings a relationship grows, one in which the child dares to take a chance, to make mistakes, and to ask. Our mentors are chosen for attitude and aptitude, carefully trained, and given regular professional guidance.

Building the capacities that will matter

Alongside foundations in language and mathematics, the Learning Bus builds central future capacities: creativity, communication, critical and connected thinking, emotional intelligence, and the ability to regulate oneself. Children learn to see how things connect, to make decisions on their own, to meet difficulty constructively, and to stay with a task even when it is not the appealing one. This strengthens not only school competence but also important conditions for taking part in society and for learning throughout life.

Early language support and access to education

Language is the foundation of learning, of belonging, and of success in education. The Learning Bus supports children who are growing up with German as a second language from the age of three onwards, as they build both everyday and academic language. Through language-rich interaction, children widen their vocabulary, improve their comprehension, and gain confidence in expressing themselves. This strengthens their ability to communicate, their active participation in education, and their trust in their own abilities.

Making learning visible

Over the course of the year, children document their ideas, their work, their questions and their discoveries in a personal Lernkiste, a learning box of their own. This makes individual development and learning visible, and open to reflection. The approach follows the strengths-based understanding of education in the Reggio Emilia tradition. What matters is not the result alone, but the path towards it. Children experience that their thoughts, their ideas and their progress are valuable and are noticed. Nothing is marked, everything is kept, and the box belongs to the child.

Open to all, made possible for many

Families can book the Learning Bus. Contributions follow the family's income, and reduced fees and free places make sure that every child can take part, even when money at home is tight. We fund the free places through investment in Lerngesellschaft. When you invest in a place, you give a child time with a person who believes in them.

A girl is working on a mathematics problem that seemed unsolvable to her a few weeks ago. She thinks, discards one approach, tries again, and finally finds her own way to the answer. In that moment more than a subject goal is reached: the child experiences herself as capable and able to act.

When self-efficacy becomes real

Experiences of self-efficacy like this one are at the heart of the Learning Bus. Children recognise that their effort has an effect, that learning is a process of development, and that difficulty can be met through persistence, curiosity and their own thinking. Out of this grows a positive and lasting relationship to learning.

Who runs it

The Learning Bus is one of Lerngesellschaft's three paths.

Lerngesellschaft is an association (Verein) based in Basel. It fosters the natural curiosity and the will to learn of children and young people along three paths: the Forscherbühne, the Learning Bus, and Brain and Society.
