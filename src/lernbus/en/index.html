Why the Lernbus

We come before a child decides there is something they cannot do.

Most support programmes begin once something has already gone wrong: after poor marks, when a child has already formed a view of themselves. That view is more stubborn than any rule of arithmetic. The Lernbus begins before that, in the years that decide how a child feels about learning. It is open to every child, whatever they bring with them and wherever they come from.

  • Children learn from people, not from programmes.

    That is why the same person stays at your child's side for months: someone who knows their name, their detours and last week's open question.

  • We begin with what a child can already do.

    Every child is good at something: building towers, inventing stories, looking closely. From a strength you dare to approach something difficult; from a weakness, rarely.

  • Mistakes are allowed. Otherwise nobody dares to try anything of their own.

    A mistake shows where to go next. Nothing in the Lernbus is graded, and what a child has tried out for themselves, they truly understand.

How it works

Seven stops, one journey.

From the first conversation to a child's own Lernkiste. Everything starts with getting to know each other, with no obligation.

    Getting to know each other

    From September 2026
  2. 2

    The right person

    Your child is matched with a learning mentor who suits their interests, and keeps that person for the whole time.

  3. 3

    60 to 90 minutes every week

The Lernhaltestelle

We start at Münsterplatz 17 in Basel: a fixed, quiet place in the middle of the city, where children arrive and are glad to come back. Later the bus itself joins in and drives into the neighbourhoods. The way to learning should be short.

What your child does with us

Build, count, tell stories, look closely.

There is no screen in the Lernbus. We work with books, paper and pencils, with a magnifying glass, scales and clay, and with whatever can be found outside. What a child has worked out for themselves stays.

The content follows your child's interests and strengths. Reading, writing and arithmetic are not left out: where a child gets stuck, we practise it along the way. We accompany children who are still building up their German from the age of three, playfully, through stories, songs and conversation. And everything a child has thought, tried and discovered goes into their Lernkiste. It belongs to the child.

  • Magnifying glass looking closely
  • Scales comparing and counting
  • Clay shaping and staying with it
  • Books language and stories
  • Pencils and paper ideas of your own
  • Chestnuts and leaves discovering outside
  • Paper planes trying things out, mistakes allowed

One session, for example

  1. Arriving and telling what happened this week.
  2. Investigating something: with the magnifying glass, the scales or clay, depending on what is on the child's mind.
  3. Reading, writing, counting, wherever it fits in.
  4. What came out of it goes into the Lernkiste.

For you

In the Lernbus you may ask as many questions as you like. You build, count, tell stories and look very closely. If something goes wrong, that is fine. You just try again. Everything you discover goes into your Lernkiste. And that one is yours. What will you put in first?

Who works with your child

People who stay.

Our learning mentors come from different professions, work reflectively and with empathy, and are chosen with care. So every child finds a person who can connect with their interests.

The team

Meet the team

Quality that is checked

Team meetings, professional exchange, evaluation and further training safeguard the quality of the support and keep developing it. The concept follows the Reggio Emilia approach: children actively shape their own learning, and their paths are made visible rather than graded.

What it costs

A clear price. And places made possible.

CHF 100per hour

A session lasts 60 to 90 minutes and therefore costs CHF 100 to 150. With one session a week, that comes to around CHF 430 to 650 a month. A reduced rate applies for pairs. The support runs for at least six months, because trust takes time. Everything else we discuss when we meet.

Places made possible

Some places are made possible by people who invest in children. If money is tight at home, simply say so. It is not an obstacle, and it stays between us.

Frequent questions

      
What parents ask us.

      

        
Is this tutoring?
Not in the usual sense. We work on gaps where there are gaps. Above all, though, it is about your child learning gladly and independently. That lasts longer than any single topic.

        
Does my child need to have problems?
No. The Lernbus is open to every child, whatever they bring with them and wherever they come from. We deliberately begin early, to preserve and strengthen confidence and the joy of learning.

        
Is this not too early?
Young children learn through play and within a secure relationship, and that is exactly where we begin. The point is that learning stays joined to joy from the start.

        
Why at least six months?
Because trust takes time. The effect does not come from any single exercise, but from a reliable relationship with a person who is genuinely enthusiastic about your child and their path of learning.

        
One child or two?
Both are possible. Some children concentrate better alone, others come alive with a second child there, because explaining and listening are themselves ways of learning. What suits your child we decide together when we meet.

        
My child speaks little German so far.
Then you are in the right place. Early German support begins at the age of three, playfully, through stories, songs and conversation, again one child or two at a time.

        
When and where do the sessions take place?
At the Lernhaltestelle at Münsterplatz 17 in Basel, usually once a week. We agree the times together. Later the Lernbus will drive into the neighbourhoods.

        
And during the school holidays?
In the holidays there are small groups for children the Lernbus accompanies. They deepen and consolidate what has grown during the term.
