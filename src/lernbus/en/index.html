Learning support

Long-term support.

We support children over an extended period. They attend one learning session each week, either individually or in a small group. This gives them stability through transitions, such as starting kindergarten or primary school. Because we know the children well, we can follow their development and continually adapt our support. Our aim is to help children build lasting core skills and learn, step by step, to work independently.

A bridge between child, family and school.

Lernbus connects each child with their parents, kindergarten and school. Where needed, we involve other important people in the child’s life or specialist services. We work closely with parents and coordinate our support with them. This brings together the experiences and observations of everyone involved. Together, we clarify goals and connect our support with the child’s everyday life. The approaches we develop together can then help at home and at school too.

Learning rests on many foundations.

That is why we foster movement and perception, language, attention, and emotional and social skills. At the same time, we develop core skills in mathematics, reading and writing, and consolidate them through focused practice. We combine play and discovery with tasks suited to each child.

We show children what helps them learn.

Together, we develop learning strategies: breaking tasks into small steps, trying different ways to solve a problem, making use of help and reviewing how they approached a task. Children discover that they can learn what they cannot do yet, through practice, good strategies and support. We take their feelings seriously, establish clear routines and stay alongside them when learning is hard. In this way, we strengthen their confidence, enjoyment of learning and independence.

How it works

  • Introductory meeting with the parents and the child

  • Weekly learning support in 60-minute sessions, individually or in a small group. Booked for one school semester at a time.

  • Building and consolidating core skills: movement and perception, language, attention, mathematics, reading and writing

  • A safe learning environment built on trust

  • A personal learning box (Lernkiste) to document each child's progress

  • Parent meetings and support tailored to the needs of children and parents, including accompanying them to meetings

  • Small groups during school holidays to strengthen and develop a wide range of skills

Frequently asked questions

Is this tutoring?

We also consolidate what your child learns at school and work on gaps where they exist. At the same time, we strengthen the foundations for learning. This helps your child enjoy learning and work independently.

Does my child need to be struggling?

No. Lernbus is open to every child, whatever their starting point or background. We begin early to preserve and strengthen their confidence and enjoyment of learning.

Is it too early to start?

Young children learn through play and through secure relationships. That is where we begin: helping them enjoy learning from the outset, in many different ways.

Why book for a school semester?

Booking for a school semester gives us time to get to know each other and keep working on suitable tasks. We combine a reliable relationship with targeted support. Lessons are booked for a school semester at a time.

Who works with my child?

Our mentors have training in education and experience of working with children. They are carefully selected to meet the highest standards. Regular team meetings, shared professional learning, evaluation and continuing professional development help us maintain quality.

Meet the team →

Individual sessions or a small group?

Your child attends one learning session each week, individually or in a small group. We discuss together which format suits them. The preparatory semester has separate groups for starting kindergarten and starting school, with four children in each group. You book one school semester at a time.

And during the school holidays?

During the school holidays, small groups help children who attend Lernbus build on and consolidate what they have learnt during the school semester.

Professional standards and quality assurance

Our mentors bring empathy, reflective practice and professional expertise to their work. Their educational training and experience enable them to respond to each child's interests and needs.

We maintain and continually improve the quality of the programme.

Who is behind it

A programme of Lerngesellschaft.

Lerngesellschaft (Learning Society) is a non-profit association. It nurtures the natural curiosity and desire to learn of children and young people. Its board members serve on a voluntary basis.
